# Privacy Policy (Demo)

**Last updated:** May 2026

Riverside Community Credit Union (“RCCU,” “we”) operates this portfolio demonstration site. It is **not** a live financial institution.

## Information we collect

On this demo site we do not collect real member data. In a production environment, RCCU would collect information you provide through forms, online banking, and cookies used for analytics—with notices at the point of collection.

## How information is used

Production uses would include servicing accounts, fraud prevention, regulatory reporting, and improving digital experiences. This demo does not process payments or store credentials.

## Sharing

RCCU would not sell personal information. Service providers (hosting, analytics, core processing) would operate under contracts limiting use to permitted purposes.

## Your choices

Members would access privacy preferences in online banking and could contact us at digital@example.rccu.demo.
